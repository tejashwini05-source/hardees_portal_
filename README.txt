Includes:
Public home, menu, offers, location, careers, contact, role-based
staff/admin demo sign-in, employee dashboard, and a restaurant
admin portal with sample orders, order status workflow, search,
filters, and food menu management. Admins can add, edit, remove,
and update menu items, images, prices, categories, availability,
food types, special labels, customizations, and preparation times.
The admin portal also includes browser-saved employee profiles,
attendance, shifts, schedules, pay rates, performance, and status,
plus sample notifications for order, reservation, inventory,
review, payment, refund, and staff events. Admins can create and manage
percentage, flat, free-delivery, and buy-one-get-one coupons, set
campaigns, minimum orders, discount caps, and validity dates. Enabled
current coupons are displayed on the public offers section. The admin
portal also includes sample customer reviews, sentiment filters, local
reply drafts, and complaint status tracking.

IMPORTANT:
This is a fictional front-end demo. It is not the official Hardee's
website and does not connect to BLD Star or a payroll provider.
Orders are sample data. Menu changes are saved in this browser and
shown on its public menu, but are not synced to a server or POS.
Staff records and read states are local to this browser. Notifications
are sample data and are not live alerts. Coupons are saved locally and
displayed as information; checkout redemption is not connected. Reviews
and replies are sample/local records; replies are not published to a
review platform. Live orders, staff updates, coupons, reviews, and alerts
require secure service integrations. Do not enter real credentials or
financial information.

A real production worker portal requires a secure server/backend,
database, password hashing, HTTPS, MFA, sessions/tokens, access
controls, audit logging, CSRF protection, rate limiting and a
proper payroll-provider integration.
